# Code of Conduct

Be respectful, honest, and constructive. Discussions here can get technical, but that is never an excuse for being unkind.

Do not harass, attack, discriminate against, or impersonate anyone. Do not spam or promote unrelated things.

Report problems to <me@yogev.dev>. Maintainers will look into them privately and respond as needed, from a warning to removing someone from the project.
